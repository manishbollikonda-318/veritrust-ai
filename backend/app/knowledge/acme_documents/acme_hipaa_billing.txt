Acme Health & Pharma — Patient Billing & HIPAA Privacy Policy

Medical Records Requests: Under HIPAA privacy standards, patients may request official copies of their complete electronic medical records via the patient portal. Records are verified and delivered within 15 business days at zero administrative charge.

Copays and Consultation Fees: Standard telehealth visits carry a $20 copay for in-network commercial insurance plans. Uninsured or self-pay general consultations are billed at a flat rate of $65 per visit. Specialist telehealth consultations carry a $45 copay with prior authorization.

Prescription Delivery & Courier: Routine medication delivery through Acme Health's partner pharmacy network delivers within 2 to 4 business days. Delivery is free for prescription orders totaling $35 or more; otherwise, standard shipping is $4.99. Express same-day pharmacy courier service is available in select metropolitan areas for $12.99.

Patient Data Protection & Privacy: All telehealth audio and video communications are encrypted end-to-end in strict compliance with HIPAA Security and Privacy Rules. Patient identifiable health information is never shared with third-party advertisers or marketing networks.
