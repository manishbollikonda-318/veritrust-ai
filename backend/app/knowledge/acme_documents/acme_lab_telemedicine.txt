Acme Health & Pharma — Laboratory Orders & Diagnostic Testing Policy

Diagnostic Testing Network: Laboratory testing and blood draw orders issued by Acme Health clinicians are valid for 60 calendar days from the date of consultation. Patients may fulfill diagnostic orders at any certified Quest Diagnostics or Labcorp partner facility nationwide with no appointment needed.

Pediatric & Minor Consultation Eligibility: Telehealth consultations for minor dependents under the age of 18 require the documented presence and consent of an authorized parent or legal guardian throughout the entire video examination. Clinicians will decline consultations where a minor is unattended.

State Licensure & Geographic Jurisdiction: By federal and state medical board statutes, Acme Health physicians may only evaluate and treat patients physically situated in states where the clinician possesses an active medical license at the time of the encounter. Patients must verify their physical location prior to connecting.
