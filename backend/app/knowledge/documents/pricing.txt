NovaMart Pricing and Promotions

Price Match Guarantee: We do not currently offer a price match guarantee against competitors. Our prices are fixed as listed on our website.

Discount Codes: Only one discount code can be applied per order. Discount codes cannot be applied to clearance items or combined with other promotional offers.

Sales Tax: Applicable sales tax will be added to your order based on your shipping address.

Student Discount: We offer a 10% discount for currently enrolled students. Verification through StudentBeans is required at checkout.
