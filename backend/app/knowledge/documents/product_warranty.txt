NovaMart Product Warranty

NovaMart stands behind the quality of the products we sell.

Electronics Warranty: All electronics sold by NovaMart come with a standard 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. This warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.

Accessories Warranty: Non-electronic accessories (such as cases, cables, and bags) are covered by a 90-day limited warranty against manufacturing defects.

Warranty Claims: To make a warranty claim, please contact our customer service team with your order number and photos of the defect. We may require you to ship the defective item back to us (at our expense) for inspection before a replacement or refund is issued.
