NovaMart Return Policy

At NovaMart, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Our return policy is designed to be straightforward and fair.

Standard Return Window: Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The items must be in their original condition, unworn, unwashed, or unused, with all original tags and packaging intact.

Exceptions to the 30-day window:
- Electronics must be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- Clearance items and personalized products are final sale and cannot be returned.

Return Shipping: Returns are free if you use our pre-paid return label. If you choose to use your own shipping method, you will be responsible for the return shipping costs.

Refund Process: Once we receive and inspect your returned item, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days. Funds will be returned to the original payment method.
