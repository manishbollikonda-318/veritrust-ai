NovaMart Shipping Policy

We offer several shipping options to ensure you get your items when you need them.

Standard Shipping: Orders with standard shipping typically arrive within 5-7 business days. Standard shipping costs $5.99 for orders under $50.

Free Shipping: We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $50 (before taxes and after discounts). 

Express Shipping: For faster delivery, choose Express Shipping at checkout. Express orders arrive within 2-3 business days and cost $15.99 regardless of order value.

Next Day Delivery: Available in select metropolitan areas for $24.99. Orders must be placed before 2 PM local time.

Order Processing: All orders require 1-2 business days for processing before they are shipped out. This processing time is not included in the estimated delivery times above.
