Apex NeoBank — High-Yield Cash Account & FDIC Insurance Coverage

Interest Yield & Calculation: The Apex High-Yield Cash Account offers a variable Annual Percentage Yield (APY) of 4.85%, calculated on the daily collected ledger balance and credited on the final business day of each calendar month. There are no minimum balance requirements or monthly tier thresholds to earn this rate.

FDIC Insurance Through Program Sweep: Customer funds are swept into an FDIC-insured partner bank network. Deposits are eligible for aggregate FDIC insurance coverage up to $2,000,000 for individual accounts and $4,000,000 for joint accounts ($250,000 maximum per partner banking institution).

Fee Schedule & Overdraft Policy: Apex NeoBank charges $0 monthly maintenance fees, $0 minimum balance penalty fees, and $0 overdraft fees. Outgoing transactions that would cause a negative balance are declined automatically without assessing overdraft charges.
